# Glossary

## Mean

The average value.

## Median

The middle value after sorting.

## Standard Deviation, SD

A measure of how spread out individual observations are.

## Standard Error of the Mean, SEM

A measure of uncertainty around the estimated mean.

## Replicate

A repeated measurement.

## Technical Replicate

Repeated measurement from the same biological sample or condition.

## Biological Replicate

Independent biological sample or experiment.

## Control Group

The baseline group used for comparison.

## Treatment Group

The group receiving a treatment, drug, or condition being tested.

## p-value

A statistical value related to how surprising the observed result is under a null hypothesis.

A p-value does not prove biological importance.

## t-test

A statistical test used to compare the means of two groups.

## ANOVA

Analysis of variance. A statistical method used to compare means across more than two groups.

## Dose-response

A relationship between the amount of treatment and the measured response.

## IC50

The concentration at which a response is reduced by about 50% in a dose-response context.

In this starter kit, IC50-style fitting is for learning only.

## Outlier

A value that looks unusually different from other values.

Outliers should be reviewed carefully before any decision is made.

## Fold Change

A ratio comparing one condition to another.

Example:

```text
treatment mean / control mean
```

## log2 Fold Change

A transformed version of fold change.

```text
log2 fold change > 0 means increase
log2 fold change < 0 means decrease
```

## Heatmap

A color-based table that shows patterns in a matrix of values.

## PCA

Principal component analysis. A method for visualizing patterns in high-dimensional data.

In this kit, PCA is used only as a toy exploratory visualization.

## Housekeeping Gene

A gene expected to remain relatively stable across conditions.

## Biomarker

A measurable biological feature that may indicate a condition, response, or biological process.

## Bioinformatics

The use of computational tools to analyze biological data.

## Synthetic Data

Data created for learning or testing, not from a real experiment.
