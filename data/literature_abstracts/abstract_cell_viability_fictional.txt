Title:
A fictional compound decreases cell viability in a dose-dependent assay

Abstract:
This fictional study investigated the effect of Compound Z on cell viability across multiple concentrations. Cells were exposed to increasing concentrations of the compound, and viability was measured using a plate-based assay. The average viability decreased as concentration increased, suggesting a possible dose-response pattern in the synthetic dataset. However, the study did not include enough biological replicates to estimate potency confidently, and no orthogonal assay was used to confirm the result.

Expected topic: cell viability / dose response
Expected method: plate-based viability assay
Expected limitation: limited replicates and no orthogonal validation
