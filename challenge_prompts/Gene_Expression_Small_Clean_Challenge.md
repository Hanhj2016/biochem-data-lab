# Gene Expression Small Clean Challenge

## Mission

Use a small toy gene expression dataset to practice fold change and careful biological wording.

## Dataset Path

```text
data/gene_expression/gene_expression_small_clean.csv
```

## Tasks

1. Preview the gene expression table.
2. Calculate control mean and treatment mean for each gene.
3. Calculate fold change and log2 fold change.
4. Sort genes by log2 fold change.
5. Write a cautious interpretation of which genes are higher or lower in treatment samples.

## Bonus Task

Mark which genes look relatively stable and explain why stable genes can be useful in teaching examples.

## Expected Visual Output

A sorted bar chart with genes on the x-axis and log2 fold change on the y-axis.
