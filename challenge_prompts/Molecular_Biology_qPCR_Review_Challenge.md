# Molecular Biology qPCR Review Challenge

## Mission

Act as a molecular biology lab trainee checking synthetic qPCR Ct replicate quality before interpretation.

## Dataset Path

```text
data/career_scenarios/molecular_biology_qpcr_review.csv
```

## Tasks

1. Preview the condition, gene, gene type, replicate, Ct, and review note columns.
2. Summarize mean Ct, SD, and replicate count by condition and gene.
3. Identify target genes with larger Ct replicate spread.
4. Create a bar chart of Ct SD by gene and condition.
5. Write a cautious note about why qPCR replicate spread matters.

## Bonus Task

Explain why a housekeeping gene is included and what else real qPCR would need before interpretation.

## Expected Visual Output

A Ct replicate-spread bar chart with review status highlighted.
